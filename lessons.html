Services

Voice, breath & body.

Individual sessions in Copenhagen and online, 50 minutes each. Whether you want to build vocal technique, explore your voice in a gentler way, or simply slow down and breathe, we'll shape each session around what you need.

Vocal Coaching

Lessons combine technical vocal training with body awareness, breathing, movement, imagination, and creative expression. Depending on your needs, we can work on vocal technique, confidence, musicality, performance, vocal freedom, or simply finding more comfort in your own voice.

Whether you are a beginner discovering your voice for the first time, an experienced singer wishing to refine your skills, or someone who simply loves to sing, the lessons are tailored to your individual goals and pace. Together, we will explore your voice in a healthy, sustainable, and supportive environment, where curiosity, playfulness, and authenticity are just as important as technique.

The goal is not to become someone else's voice, but to discover your own.

Vocal Exploration

Vocal Exploration is for those who want to explore their voice beyond singing technique. Through sound, breath, and gentle movement, we create space to notice, feel, and express what is present in the body.

In a session, we might explore releasing a thought, belief, or feeling through vocalisation, allowing a sound to emerge, exist in the body, and find its way into expression. Rather than focusing on how the voice should sound, we explore what happens when we give it space to be heard.

In a safe space, inspired by Paul Newham's Voice Movement Therapy — the idea that everything that happens in our lives leaves a trace on our voice, and that the voice can also be a way out — I draw from my own work with voice and breathwork to guide and support you in this process.

The intention is to encourage freer vocal expression, a deeper connection with yourself, and greater comfort in using your voice, without needing to get anything right.

For anyone looking for a different way into vocal work — who feels stuck, or is looking for a safe place to express their voice and find comfort through it.

Breathwork & Meditation

These one-to-one sessions are for those who want to slow down, find grounding, and feel more at ease in themselves. Through gentle breathing practices, meditation, and body awareness, we create space to pause, reconnect with the present moment, and listen to what the body needs.

My approach is gentle and guided by your individual needs, with an emphasis on feeling safe, comfortable, and supported throughout the practice. Drawing on my training in Breathwork & Meditation, I guide you in exploring your breath at your own pace, without pressure to achieve a particular state or experience.

The intention is to support relaxation, greater body awareness, and a more settled relationship with yourself, giving you space to simply be.
